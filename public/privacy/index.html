Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

ClaimSheet helps you build a contents list for a home insurance claim. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we use your information to run ClaimSheet, and we don't sell or share it with anyone else.

What we collect

  • Your list. The rooms, items, descriptions, brands, ages, quantities and prices you enter, plus any claim number or notes you add.
  • Your photos. Pictures you upload of your belongings.
  • Payment details. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us whether the payment went through, plus basic details like the amount and the email you entered at checkout.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use these to keep the site running and to stop abuse.

You don't need an account to use ClaimSheet. Your list is tied to a private link instead of a username and password.

How we use it

  • To save your list and photos so you can come back to them.
  • To fill in item details from a photo when you use the AI photo scan.
  • To build your PDF and Excel packet.
  • To process payments, provide refunds and answer your questions.
  • To keep ClaimSheet secure and working.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We only use a few service providers to run ClaimSheet:

  • Netlify hosts the website and stores your list and photos.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that reads a photo when you use the photo scan. The photo is sent to identify the item and is not used to train their models.
  • Stripe processes payments.

We may also share information if the law requires it, such as a valid subpoena or court order.

Your private link

Anyone who has your private link can open your list. Keep it to yourself, and only share it with people you trust, like your adjuster or a family member helping you.

How long we keep it

We keep your list and photos so you can come back to them during your claim. If you want your list and photos deleted, email cmrutherford3@gmail.com with your private link and we'll delete them, usually within 7 days. Payment records are kept by Stripe as required for tax and accounting.

Children

ClaimSheet is meant for adults handling their own insurance claims. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email cmrutherford3@gmail.com.
