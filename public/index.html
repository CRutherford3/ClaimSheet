Why I built this

I spent years as a property claims adjuster. I've been the person on the other side of the contents list, and I've seen firsthand what happens when people don't have one.

After a fire, a flood or a storm, families get handed a blank spreadsheet and asked to remember everything they owned: what it was, what brand, how old, and what it costs to replace today. It's the worst possible time to do homework. So the list comes back short and vague. "TV." "Couch." "Clothes."

An adjuster can only pay for what's on the list, and only as well as it's described.

When an item has no detail, it usually gets priced as the most basic version. Things people forget to list don't get paid at all. Not because anyone is trying to shortchange them, but because there's nothing to go on.

The people who came out best had the details: brand, model, age, photos and a real replacement price. Their claims moved faster and paid closer to what they actually lost.

ClaimSheet is the tool I wish I could have handed every policyholder. Snap a photo, check the details, and send your adjuster a list in the format they actually work from.

Common questions

Will my insurance company accept this?

Most insurance companies ask for an itemized list of damaged belongings with descriptions, ages and values. ClaimSheet gives you exactly that, in the format adjusters are used to. Your insurer still reviews it and sets final values under your policy.

How accurate is the photo fill-in?

It's good at recognizing what an item is, and it reads brands and model numbers when they're visible. Prices are suggestions based on what a comparable new item typically costs, so check each one. There's a button to look the item up online.

Do I need an account?

No. Your list gets a private link. Save it, text it to yourself, and use it to open your list on any phone or computer.

Is my information private?

Yes. We don't sell or share your information. Your list and photos are stored securely and can only be opened with your private link, so keep it to yourself. The only time anything leaves ClaimSheet is to do the job: a photo is read by our AI service to fill in the item, and payments go through Stripe. Neither one gets to keep or use your information for anything else.

What if I add more items after I pay?

Add as many as you need and download a fresh packet. Your packet stays unlocked.

Is ClaimSheet a public adjuster?

No. ClaimSheet helps you document and organize your belongings. It doesn't negotiate with your insurance company or give legal advice.

How do I get help or a refund?

Email support@claimsheet.io and include your private link. If you paid and haven't downloaded your packet yet, you can get a full refund within 7 days. See the Refund Policy for details.
